# Files

- [Answer Evaluators](answer-evaluators.md) - The generation-side evaluators in evaluators.py — deterministic format_validator checks, abstention detection via normalized marker families, abstention_summary's recall/over-refusal reporting with Wilson CIs, and the token-F1 summary evaluator — plus the LangSmith evaluator contract they follow.
- [Experiments & Decision Gates](decision-gates.md) - The checkpoint-and-experiment governance model — the CKPT-0 through CKPT-8 ladder, the EXP series each ending in a PROMOTE/REJECT gate, the decision log, ADR conventions, and the rule that every failure point gets an evaluator, a slice, an experiment, and a production metric.
- [Golden Dataset](golden-dataset.md) - The arxiv-copilot-golden LangSmith dataset — the uniform example schema, two-axis provenance model, six slices and their construction methods, synthetic-by-construction generation, and the idempotent sync contract in build_golden_dataset.py.
- [Retrieval Metrics](retrieval-metrics.md) - The retrieval metrics in evaluators.py — paper-level vs chunk-level comparison, hit/recall/MRR/precision semantics, the gold-completeness gate on precision, the per-example reporting policy, and the anti-pooling-bias design verified against the ir-measures oracle.
